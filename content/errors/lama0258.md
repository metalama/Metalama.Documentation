# LAMA0258: The declaration can't be a template

| | |
|---|---|
| **Severity** | Error |
| **Reported by** | Metalama.Framework |
| **Message** | `'<Declaration>' cannot be a template because constructors, finalizers, operators, and conversion operators are not supported as templates. Introduce the member programmatically from a template method instead.` |

## Cause

You can introduce fields, properties, events, and methods declaratively by marking a member of the aspect with [<xref:Metalama.Framework.Aspects.IntroduceAttribute>], or turn such a member into a template with [<xref:Metalama.Framework.Aspects.TemplateAttribute>]. Finalizers, user-defined operators, and conversion operators can't be templates, because their declaration in the aspect class is bound to the aspect type itself. The same rule applies to constructors.

Metalama reports LAMA0258 when one of these members carries a template attribute.

## Resolution

Write the body of the member in a regular method marked with [<xref:Metalama.Framework.Aspects.TemplateAttribute>], and introduce the member programmatically from `BuildAspect` with the matching advice method: `IntroduceFinalizer`, `IntroduceUnaryOperator`, `IntroduceBinaryOperator`, `IntroduceConversionOperator`, or `IntroduceConstructor`.
