# LAMA1013: The interceptor method isn't compatible with the call site

| | |
|---|---|
| **Severity** | Error |
| **Reported by** | Metalama.Extensions.Interceptors |
| **Message** | `The method '<Method>' cannot intercept the <Site> because <Reason>.` |

## Cause

The `Metalama.Extensions.Interceptors` package lets an aspect or a fabric replace the calls to selected methods with calls to another method, called an _interceptor_. With `RedirectToExistingMethod`, the interceptor is a method that you've written. Metalama replaces the call with a call to this method, and the compiler must be able to bind the new call.

Metalama reports LAMA1013 when the method doesn't satisfy this requirement. In the message, `<Method>` is the proposed interceptor, `<Site>` describes the intercepted call, and `<Reason>` gives the incompatibility. The interceptor method must, among others:

* be static;
* be accessible from the call site;
* have a signature that matches the intercepted method: the parameters, their types and `ref`, `in` and `out` modifiers, and the return type;
* receive the receiver as its first parameter when the intercepted method is an instance method;
* be a run-time method, not a compile-time method.

Metalama also reports LAMA1013 for a base call to a virtual method, because a static method would call the method with a virtual dispatch and reach the override.

## Resolution

Fix the interceptor method according to the reason given in the message. In the following example, the interceptor method is made static.

Alternatively, use `RedirectToSynthesizedMethod` and let Metalama generate the interceptor method from a template: the signature is then derived from the intercepted method.
