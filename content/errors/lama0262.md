# LAMA0262: Template or advice attributes can't be applied to an accessor

| | |
|---|---|
| **Severity** | Error |
| **Reported by** | Metalama.Framework |
| **Message** | `Accessor '<Accessor>' cannot have the '<Attribute>' attribute. Add the attribute to the containing <MemberKind> instead.` |

## Cause

Template attributes such as <xref:Metalama.Framework.Aspects.TemplateAttribute> and advice attributes such as <xref:Metalama.Framework.Aspects.IntroduceAttribute> apply to a whole property or event, including its accessors.

Metalama reports LAMA0262 when one of these attributes is applied to an accessor, such as `get`, `set`, `add`, or `remove`. The message gives the accessor, the attribute, and the kind of the containing member (`property` or `event`).

In the following example, the `[Introduce]` attribute is applied to the getter of the `SchemaVersion` property.

[!metalama-test ~/code/Metalama.Documentation.SampleCode.Errors/LAMA0262/LAMA0262_Error.cs name="Error"]

## Resolution

Move the attribute from the accessor to the containing property or event, as in the following example.

If the property or event already has a template or advice attribute, remove the one on the accessor. Otherwise, Metalama also reports <xref:lama0261>.
